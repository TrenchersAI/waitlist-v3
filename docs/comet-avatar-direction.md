# Comet spirits — twilight series

Visual reference: *Your Name* (2016). Reviewed the six stills in the [official GKIDS gallery](https://gkids.com/films/your-name/) and sampled its [official trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RuyHIkXdYf8). IMDb was inaccessible during research.

The direction draws from the split blue/rose comet, luminous white centers, fine particle trails, and the violet/peach light in the landscape stills. The characters are original vector/canvas artwork; no movie images or footage were added to the repository.

- **Aster:** cyan pearl core, rose secondary trail.
- **Ember:** rose pearl core, blue secondary trail.
- **Dusk:** violet pearl core, turquoise secondary trail.

Each spirit has a rounded four-point silhouette, two curved trails, a small companion fragment, and drifting particles. Bot Avatars supplies the facial expressions and gaze simulation. The component is available in `/avatar-lab`; the landing hero displays the generated landscape without bot avatars.

Idle motion is slow; working brightens and speeds up the particles; sleeping dims and shortens the trails. Animation is capped at about 30 fps, pauses when hidden or outside the viewport, supports an explicit pause control, and becomes static for reduced-motion preferences. The hero uses an original generated comet-night landscape; see [the artwork prompt](hero-artwork-prompt.md).
